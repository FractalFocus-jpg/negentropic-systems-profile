Research focus

01 / EXECUTION

Reliability & recovery

Investigating how software preserves useful work across interruptions, with explicit permissions and testable recovery behavior.

02 / EVALUATION

Decision integrity

Examining how assessment pipelines handle ambiguous, invalid, or incomplete evidence without hiding unresolved cases.

03 / EVIDENCE

Reproducible comparisons

Connecting claims to their methods, inputs, outputs, and limitations so that another reviewer can inspect what a result actually establishes.

Evidence before claims.

My work combines systems design, software prototypes, and scoped experiments. The working method is to define the task and comparison conditions, measure the outcome, and retain failures and inconclusive results alongside successes.

Local engineering results are distinguished from independent validation and production performance. Specific claims need their corresponding evidence; a research overview is not a performance certification.

A focused route to collaboration.

I welcome technical conversations about evaluation integrity, computational reliability, and recovery. A useful starting point is one clearly defined problem, an agreed comparison method, and a bounded review scope.

This is a non-confidential overview. Private source code, unpublished mechanisms, experimental datasets, and implementation details are not provided here. Technical access and any further disclosure are agreed separately.
